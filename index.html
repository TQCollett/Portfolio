About Us

Jube's Aquariums is a locally owned aquarium store dedicated to bringing the beauty and tranquility of aquatic life to homes and offices. Known for its friendly service and expert advice, Jube's offers a wide selection of freshwater and saltwater fish, aquatic plants, corals, and custom aquariums. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned aquarist, Jube's provides everything you need—from quality equipment and maintenance services to rare fish and curated aquascaping supplies. With a passion for aquatic life and a commitment to customer satisfaction, Jube's Aquariums is more than a store—it's a community for fish lovers.

Contact Us

Have questions or need help with your aquarium setup? We're here to help! Reach out to Jube's Aquariums by phone, email, or stop by the store—we'd love to chat. Whether you’re looking for expert advice, product availability, or just want to talk fish, our friendly team is always ready to assist.

Happy Fish Guaranteed

At Jube's Aquariums, the health and happiness of our fish come first—that's why we proudly offer our Happy Fish Guarantee. Every fish we sell is carefully selected, well-cared for, and acclimated to ensure it's thriving before it reaches your tank. If your new aquatic friend isn't doing well within the first few days, we'll work with you to make it right—whether that means a replacement, store credit, or expert advice to get things back on track. Your success as a fishkeeper is our priority, and we stand behind the quality of every finned friend we send home.
